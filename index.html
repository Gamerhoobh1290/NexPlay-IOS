Welcome To NexPlay

Bring your songs in, then start playing right away.

Import music from your device, click any track to play it, and use favorites, playlists, right-click actions, and simple settings as you go. This screen is here to help first-time users get moving fast.

1. Import your music

Click Import Songs or drag files into the window. NexPlay accepts common audio formats like MP3, M4A, FLAC, WAV, AAC, OGG, and Opus. For the cleanest titles and artists, it is best to name files like Artist - Song.

Best results: use Artist - Song. Tip: start with 3 to 5 songs for your first test.

2. Play and organize

Click any track to play it. Use the heart to save favorites, the playlist button to group songs, and the mini-player at the bottom to pause, skip, and scrub.

Everything is written in plain actions, so new users do not need a manual.

3. Learn the power tools

Right click a track for quick actions. Press Ctrl/Cmd + K for commands. Turn on Multi-Select when you want to queue, delete, or organize many tracks at once.

Settings lets people tune theme, playback, resume, and more.

Best First Run

Import a few songs, click one to start playback, right click another track to explore actions, then open Settings if you want to change the theme or playback behavior.

Works Beyond Songs Too

If you want more than local songs, NexPlay also supports local video files and an Online Videos hub for saved video links.
